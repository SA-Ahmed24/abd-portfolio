Section 07 — Roots / Pakistan
"home is where the masala chai is hot, the cricket match is loud, and naani is calling you for dinner."

where i'm really from.

lahore, pakistan. always.

i was born in lahore on a hot august afternoon in 2004. my father is a physician, my mother a college graduate from kinnaird, both raising four kids with the same patience pakistan demands.

at seven i moved to singapore. learned chinese (passed yct grade 5), played cricket on astroturf, won "cricketer of the year" at tanglin trust school. for eight years i was a singaporean kid with a pakistani heart, samosas in my lunchbox.

at thirteen, my grandfather had a stroke. we moved back to lahore. i went to aitchison college — the school imran khan went to, the school my mother's brothers went to, the school my older brother prefected for. پرسیویرنس کمانڈز سکسیس — perseverance commands success.

now i'm in toronto. but lahore is still where i wake up in my dreams. the call to prayer at fajr, the chaiwala on the street, my uncle's kebabs at iftar.

my uncle, dr. rizwan naseer, founded rescue 1122 — pakistan's national emergency response service. that's the model i grew up with: قوم کی خدمت — service to the nation. it's in my blood.

everything i build, i build with that in mind.
